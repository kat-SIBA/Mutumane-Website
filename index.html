About Mutumane

Serving passion, flavor, and authentic local community spirit.

Our Story & Heritage

Founded with a passion for exceptional township flavor, Mutumane Kota 'N' Grilled Chicken brings together the best of both worlds: heavily loaded, authentic local kotas and juicy, perfectly seasoned flame-grilled chicken.

Quality Ingredients

We believe great food starts with top-quality ingredients. From freshly baked bread and crisp vegetables to our signature spice blends, every meal is prepared fresh to order to guarantee maximum satisfaction.

Our Mission

To serve delicious, comforting, and affordable meals that bring family, friends, and the local community together around great food.
